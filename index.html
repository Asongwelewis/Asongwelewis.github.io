AI-Driven Power Outage Prediction System

Flagship

A Cameroon-focused system that predicts and detects power outages across Yaoundé, Douala, Bamenda and Buea. It aggregates heterogeneous outage signals, applies AI classification and per-zone risk scoring, confirms real outage events with evidence, and reports measured accuracy on a real-time dashboard. FastAPI + Node.js/Express services, Socket.IO live updates, Supabase/PostgreSQL storage, Cloudflare R2 object storage and a Pytest suite — all containerized with Docker.
